Current Adventures

Columbia River

The Columbia river stretches over the entire length of Oregon and Washington. This lengthy river contains vigorous rapids and various campsites along the way. Join us for the expedition starting in Sandpoint, Idaho as we cross 2 state lines and end in Hammond, Oregon on the West Coast!

Rogue River

This short and treacherous river leads directly West to the Pacific. It is full of rapids and is suitable for those who have more experience! We have 2 campsite locations along the side of this rocky rapid. Starting near Crater Lake National Park, this trip involves very few stops before ending in Gold Beach, Oregon

Snake River

For those who want a more calm adventure, the Snake River is a great choice. This expedition will leave you breathless as you travel through the serene waters of this well-known Idaho river. Visit our 4 campsite locations along the way from Pasco, Washington to Pocatello, Idaho.
